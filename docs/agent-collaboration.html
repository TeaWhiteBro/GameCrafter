ROLE CONTRACTS

Who designs what?

How one design decision travels through the system

Illustrative handoff for a neon cyberpunk world; these are role responsibilities, not an additional benchmark result.

1 / Interpret the brief

Game design specifies characters, the encounter and exploration intent. Asset planning translates the theme into recognizable objects, construction requirements and surface choices.

2 / Ground the visual plan

New sources are generated and reviewed. Art direction examines their actual colors and available materials before assigning palette, regional functions and composition rules.

3 / Make a playable space

World design places landmarks, connected streets and a clear encounter space. Blender realizes the metric plan; UE applies animation, rendering, collision and gameplay.

4 / Correct the right cause

If current UE views lose the intended neon identity, visual review cites the images and defect. The executing agent diagnoses the responsible asset, material or lighting layer, repairs it and collects new evidence.

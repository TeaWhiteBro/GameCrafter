# Public release

GameCrafter distributes one current framework contract: fresh-source bootstrapping; prompt/reference generation; configurable Blender construction and humanoid skinning; UE motion retargeting, rendering, exploration and melee combat; three cameras; five paired observation channels; evidence-bound review/repair; hashed recovery and clean-source export.

The semantic package version is available through `python pipeline.py --version`. Validation scope is documented in [docs/VALIDATION.md](docs/VALIDATION.md). New results must identify their actual source manifest and acceptance evidence.
